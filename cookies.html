Cookie Policy.

How this website handles essential browser storage and future optional technologies.

Last updated: September 2026

This page is a website policy template and should be reviewed against the final legal, operational and data-processing setup before publication.

1. Essential storage

The current static website stores a small preference in your browser so the cookie notice does not appear repeatedly after you make a choice.

2. Optional technologies

If analytics, advertising, embedded services, appointment tools, chat, CRM integrations or similar technologies are added, they should be assessed and disclosed before activation.

3. Managing preferences

You can clear browser site data to remove locally stored preferences. Browser controls can also be used to restrict cookies, although some site functions may be affected.

4. Questions

For questions about data handling, contact info@cmfilings.com.
